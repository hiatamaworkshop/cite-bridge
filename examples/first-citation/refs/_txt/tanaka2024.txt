Typing and Speaking: A Small Study
Abstract We compared typing and speaking for short notes.
1 Introduction
People write short notes every day. Many of them now speak to their phones.
2 Method
Twelve participants wrote ten notes by typing and ten notes by speaking.
3 Results
Speaking was faster than typing, but participants corrected more words after speaking.
4 Discussion
The speed of speaking did not remove the cost of correction.
